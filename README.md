"The First Loaf" is a poem about agency, told through bread. A kitchen that bakes while its owner sleeps weighs the flour to the gram and has never burned a loaf. One morning the speaker wakes before the timer, shapes a single piece of dough by hand the way their mother taught them, and the lopsided loaf that results is the one they eat first.
